#  Discussion Etiquette
### *Building Customized LLMs with OpenAI — Columbia Plus*

Conversations are an integral part of your learning for this course. While not graded, you are encouraged to participate in weekly discussion forums to express ideas and ask questions about things you do not understand. Use this guide to ensure a civil, helpful, and encouraging community environment.

---

##  Community Guidelines
To support the best experience for all learners, please adhere to the following standards:

* **Be Polite**: We have learners from all over the world with different backgrounds. Build an encouraging community by commenting on the *ideas* of an argument, not the people they come from.
* **Upvote Good Posts**: Use the "Like" button at the bottom of posts so that high-quality questions and answers can be found more easily by others.
* **Be Accurate**: Include descriptive titles and provide as much context as possible (e.g., which video or problem you are referencing).
* **Write Clearly**: Post questions and responses in English only. Avoid using "all caps," excessive punctuation, or non-standard abbreviations to ensure everyone can understand your message.
* **Talk to Each Other**: Engage critically and thoughtfully with your classmates. It is okay to disagree or argue a point, but you MUST do so in a way that is respectful and civil.

> **Note**: Hostile or overly aggressive tones will not be tolerated. Posts that do not adhere to these guidelines will be removed from the discussion threads.
