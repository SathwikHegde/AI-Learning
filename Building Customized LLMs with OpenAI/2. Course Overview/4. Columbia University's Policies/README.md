# Columbia University's Policies
### *Building Customized LLMs with OpenAI — Columbia Plus*

Upon enrolling in this course, all learners agree to adhere to the official policies of **Columbia University**. These guidelines are established to ensure a fair, respectful, and high-quality learning environment for all current and future participants.

---

## Core Policy Areas

### **1. Rules of University Conduct**
Learners are expected to maintain professional behavior throughout the course duration. This includes respectful interaction in discussion forums and adherence to the community standards established for the Columbia Plus platform.

### **2. Academic Integrity**
To maintain the value of the Columbia Plus credential, learners must:
* Complete all quizzes and assignments independently.
* Refrain from sharing quiz answers or restricted course code with others.
* Ensure all work submitted reflects their own learning and understanding of the AI frameworks discussed.

### **3. Copyright Policy**
All course materials—including video lectures, technical PDFs (like `M4_BuildAISolutions.pdf`), and proprietary datasets—are the property of Columbia University. 
* **Restriction**: These materials are for your personal educational use only. Unauthorized distribution, reproduction, or commercial use of course content is strictly prohibited.

## Compliance & Cooperation
Adhering to these policies ensures that the certification process remains meaningful and that the collaborative spaces remain productive for everyone. Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining the standards of Columbia University.
