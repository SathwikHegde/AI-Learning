# Certificate FAQs: Building Customized LLMs with OpenAI
### *Columbia Plus — Columbia University*

This directory provides comprehensive information regarding the **Columbia Plus Certificate** awarded upon the successful completion of the "Building Customized LLMs with OpenAI" course. Use this guide to understand the value of the credential and the specific steps required to earn it.

---

## What is a Certificate?
A certificate is a professional credential signifying that you have successfully completed the course and fulfilled all rigorous academic requirements. It serves as a formal recognition of the time, effort, and technical expertise you have invested in mastering customized AI solutions.

---

## How to Earn Your Certificate
To be eligible for the credential, you must meet three primary criteria:
* **Full Engagement**: You must view all of the learning pages within the course modules.
* **Academic Mastery**: You must score **60% or higher** on all course quizzes.
* **Feedback**: You must complete a brief 5-minute post-course survey.

Once these criteria are met, you will have the option to pay for and receive your official certificate.

---

## Utilizing Your Credential
Your certificate is a powerful tool for career advancement. You can:
* **Professional Showcasing**: Display the certificate on your professional networks, such as **LinkedIn**, to enhance your profile.
* **Skill Verification**: Demonstrate your newly acquired skills in OpenAI API integration, RAG development, and AI solution architecture to current or future employers.
* **Personal Portfolio**: Include it in your personal viewing or digital resume to track your continuous learning progress.

---

## Achievement Preview

The official certificate features your name, the course title, the date of completion, and a verification QR code to ensure authenticity.

---

## Related Progress Trackers
To monitor your path toward this certificate, please refer to the following module assessments:
* **Module 4 Self-Check Questions**: Found in the `Module 4 Self-Check Question` folder.
* **Module 4 Reflection Discussion**: Participation required as part of the active learning criteria.
* **Technical Labs**: Successful completion of scripts in the `Module 4.1` through `Module 4.7` units.
