# Course FAQs: Building Customized LLMs with OpenAI
### *Columbia Plus — Columbia University*

This directory contains essential information regarding the administrative and technical requirements for the **Building Customized LLMs with OpenAI** course. Use this guide to understand certification criteria, support structures, and further learning opportunities.

---

## Certification & Prerequisites
* **Prerequisites**: Successful completion requires foundational knowledge of **Python programming**.
* **Earning a Certificate**: To be eligible, you must:
    * View all learning pages within the course.
    * Score **60% or higher** on all quizzes.
    * Complete a 5-minute post-course survey.
* **University Credit**: Please note that learners **do not** earn university credit for completing this certificate.

---

## Quizzes & Assessments
* **Attempts**: You have **unlimited attempts** and time to complete all quizzes and projects.
* **Grading**: The highest score from your attempts will be counted toward your final grade.

---

## Support & Community
* **Instructor Support**: There is **no active monitoring** by instructors or course assistants for this self-directed course.
* **Peer Learning**: Learners are highly encouraged to participate in **course discussions** to ask questions and receive feedback from other participants.

---

## Technical Requirements
* **Devices**: For the best experience, use a **computer or laptop** rather than a mobile device.
* **Recommended Browsers**:
    * Google Chrome
    * Safari
    * Microsoft Edge
* **Platform Constraints**: You cannot open the same course page in multiple windows simultaneously.

---
