# Module 2 Quiz
### *Building Customized LLMs with OpenAI — Columbia Plus*

This assessment serves as the final technical check for **Module 2: Fine-Tuning LLMs**. This quiz is designed to reinforce your understanding of how to internally adapt a model's behavior, covering everything from lightweight adapters to the full OpenAI fine-tuning pipeline.

---

## Quiz Objectives
The assessment evaluates your mastery of the following technical domains:
* **Adaptation Strategies**: Distinguishing between RAG and fine-tuning use cases.
* **Efficient Customization**: Understanding adapters, LoRA, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT).
* **Data Engineering**: Mastering JSONL formatting, data cleaning, and validation logic.
* **Operational Management**: Calculating cost estimations and configuring hyperparameters like epochs and learning rates.
* **Evaluation Metrics**: Interpreting training loss and using Model IDs for inference.

## Preparation Guide
Before clicking **"Take the Quiz,"** ensure you have reviewed the following:
1.  **JSONL Standards**: Review the specific formatting requirements discussed in Unit 2.5 to avoid "garbage in, garbage out" scenarios.
2.  **Cost Math**: Be prepared to calculate estimated training costs based on token counts and epoch numbers.
3.  **Adapter Logic**: Refresh your knowledge on why adapters are more resource-efficient than full parameter fine-tuning.
