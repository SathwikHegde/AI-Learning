# Certificate Completion & Payment
### *Building Customized LLMs with OpenAI — Columbia Plus*

This section represents the final milestone of your professional journey in the **Building Customized LLMs with OpenAI** course. Once you have completed all technical modules, labs, and quizzes, you are eligible to claim your official **Columbia University digital credential**.

---

## Prerequisites for Unlocking
To gain access to the certificate issuance and payment options, you must first satisfy the administrative requirements:

* **Content Completion**: Ensure every unit across all modules (Foundations, RAG, Fine-Tuning, Multimodal AI, and Solution Deployment) is marked as "Complete."
* **Quiz Thresholds**: Verify that you have achieved the minimum required score (typically **60%**) on all module quizzes.
* **Post-Course Survey**: This is the final mandatory prerequisite. Your feedback is essential for the continuous improvement of the Columbia Plus platform and curriculum. Once the survey is submitted, the lock on the certificate section will be removed.

---

## Certificate Payment
The **Certificate Payment Page** is the portal where you finalize the administrative processing for your credential.

* **Digital Credential**: Upon successful payment, you will receive a verifiable digital certificate.
* **Verification**: These certificates are designed for professional platforms like LinkedIn, allowing employers to verify your specialized skills in LLM architecture and engineering directly through Columbia University.
* **Processing Time**: Digital credentials are typically issued immediately following the confirmation of payment and the verification of academic prerequisites.

---

## Final Steps to Certification
To ensure a smooth transition from learner to certified practitioner:
1.  **Navigate** to the **Post-Course Survey** and provide your honest feedback.
2.  **Wait** for the system to refresh (this usually happens instantly upon survey submission).
3.  **Click** on the now-unlocked **Certificate Payment Page**.
4.  **Complete** the transaction to receive your secure download link.
