# Module 1.2: Prompt Engineering Overview

### *Prompt Engineering & Programming with OpenAI*

This submodule serves as a bridge between high-level AI strategy and the practical art of interacting with modern generative models. Led by **Professor Johar**, this unit breaks down the mechanics of Large Language Models (LLMs) and the emerging discipline of Prompt Engineering.

---

## Learning Objectives

The primary goal of this overview is to demystify how AI "thinks" and how users can refine their input to achieve superior output. Key topics include:

* **The Mechanics of LLMs:** Understanding the underlying architecture that allows chatbots to generate human-like text.
* **Generative AI Fundamentals:** Exploring how models predict tokens and patterns based on massive datasets.
* **Strategic Prompting:** Learning why specific phrasing, context, and constraints are essential for business-grade AI results.

---

## Unit Content

### **Video Lecture: Fundamentals of LLMs**

Professor Johar illustrates the journey from raw data to the conversational interfaces we use today. This lecture covers:

* How modern chatbots (like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude) process natural language.
* The importance of the "Prompt" as the primary interface for non-technical business leaders.
* A breakdown of successful generative AI workflows.

---

## Skills & Knowledge Acquired

* **Model Literacy:** Ability to explain the difference between traditional search and generative AI generation.
* **Prompt Optimization:** Understanding that the quality of AI output is directly proportional to the clarity and structure of the input.
* **Operational Insight:** Recognizing how LLMs can be integrated into daily business tasks to increase productivity.
