# Module 1.6: Executing the AI Project

### *AI for Business — Columbia University*

This directory concludes the introductory module by focusing on the **Execution Phase**. It synthesizes the people, processes, and metrics discussed in previous units into a concrete action plan for launching and sustaining AI initiatives within an organization.

---

## Learning Objectives

The primary goal of this unit is to provide a blueprint for high-level execution. Key takeaways include:

* **The Pilot-to-Scale Framework:** How to start with "Quick Wins" (POCs) before committing to enterprise-wide transformations.
* **Avoiding Implementation Pitfalls:** Identifying why most AI projects fail during the transition from lab to production.
* **Resource Allocation:** Understanding how to budget time, capital, and data assets effectively.
* **Continuous Improvement:** Establishing the "MLOps" mindset—viewing AI as a living system that requires ongoing maintenance.

---

## Key Concepts Covered

### **Strategic Execution Roadmap**

The module outlines the critical path for a successful AI rollout:

1. **Selection of Use Case:** Identifying high-impact, low-complexity problems for initial success.
2. **The Proof of Concept (POC):** Building a "minimum viable model" to prove feasibility.
3. **Integration:** Embedding the AI output into existing business workflows (e.g., CRM or ERP systems).
4. **Scaling:** Expanding the solution across different departments or product lines.

### **Managing the "AI Chasm"**

A major focus of this unit is bridging the gap between a successful experiment and a reliable business tool. This includes:

* **Infrastructure Readiness:** Ensuring your cloud or on-premise servers can handle real-time AI inference.
* **Change Management:** Training non-technical staff to trust and use AI-generated insights.
* **Maintenance:** Planning for "Data Drift," where models become less accurate over time as the real world changes.

---

## Skills & Knowledge Acquired

* **Implementation Strategy:** Ability to draft an execution plan that accounts for both technical and human constraints.
* **Risk Mitigation:** Identifying technical debt and organizational resistance before they derail a project.
* **Agile AI Management:** Learning to manage the uncertainty of AI research using agile methodologies.
