# Module 1: Knowledge Check & Assignment
This directory contains the assessment materials for the conclusion of **Module 1: Course Introduction**. This assignment is designed to validate your understanding of the strategic, organizational, and ethical frameworks required to lead AI initiatives in a business environment.

## Assignment Objectives
The goal of this comprehensive assessment is to demonstrate mastery over the foundational concepts covered in Units 1.1 through 1.8:
* **Strategic Identification:** Selecting appropriate AI solutions (Generative vs. Discriminative) for specific business use cases.
* **Process Mastery:** Correctly identifying the stages in the "Sequence of Building" AI products.
* **Team Governance:** Determining the ideal team structure and roles for various project scales.
* **Ethical Literacy:** Recognizing bias, privacy concerns, and regulatory requirements in proposed AI implementations.

---

## Assessment Components

### **1. Graded Quiz**
A cumulative examination covering the core pillars of the introductory module.
* **Format:** Multiple choice and scenario-based questions.
* **Passing Threshold:** A minimum score of **60%** is required to satisfy certification requirements.
* **Policy:** Unlimited attempts are permitted; the highest score is recorded to encourage mastery over rote memorization.

### **2. Reflection Discussion**
A peer-reviewed prompt focusing on the "Build Sequence."
* **Focus:** Analyzing a real-world business problem and drafting a high-level execution plan.
* **Goal:** Synthesizing technical constraints with business KPIs.

---

## Key Topics Evaluated
The assignment specifically tests your ability to navigate:
1. **The LLM Landscape:** Understanding prompt engineering and generative model capabilities.
2. **Resource Allocation:** Choosing between Data Engineers and Data Scientists for specific pipeline tasks.
3. **Success Tracking:** Aligning technical metrics (accuracy) with business value (ROI).
4. **The Ethics Checklist:** Applying fairness and transparency filters to AI decision-making.

---

## Certification Milestone
Completing this assignment marks the official end of the **Introduction** phase. Successful completion is a prerequisite for moving into the technical deep-dives of:
* **Module 2:** ML Models
* **Module 3:** Python Basics

> **Note:** Ensure all self-check questions in Units 1.1–1.8 are marked as "Complete" before submitting the final quiz to ensure maximum preparation.
