# Module 1.3: The Sequence of Building AI Solutions

### *AI for Business — Columbia University*

This module introduces the **standardized lifecycle** of an AI project. It moves beyond the "what" of AI and dives into the "how"—providing a step-by-step roadmap for technical and business teams to collaborate effectively.

---

## Learning Objectives

The primary goal of this unit is to master the end-to-end process of AI development. Key takeaways include:

* **The Lifecycle Roadmap:** Understanding the phases from data collection to model deployment.
* **Business-Tech Alignment:** Identifying which stakeholders are responsible for each stage of the build.
* **Problem Scoping:** Learning how to define a business problem in a way that an AI model can actually solve.
* **Feasibility Assessment:** Evaluating if you have the right data and infrastructure to proceed.

---

## The "Build" Sequence

In this unit, you will explore the 5-7 core stages typically required to launch an AI system:

1. **Problem Definition:** Mapping business KPIs to AI tasks (e.g., "Reduce churn" → "Binary Classification").
2. **Data Acquisition & Cleaning:** Sourcing, labeling, and "scrubbing" data for quality.
3. **Feature Engineering:** Selecting the most relevant variables for the model.
4. **Model Selection & Training:** Choosing the right algorithm (Regression, Trees, Neural Networks, etc.).
5. **Evaluation:** Testing against business metrics, not just technical accuracy.
6. **Deployment & Monitoring:** Integrating the model into the business workflow and watching for "drift."

---

## Skills & Knowledge Acquired

* **Project Management for AI:** Ability to oversee a technical timeline without being a coder.
* **Data Strategy:** Understanding that "Garbage In, Garbage Out" is the biggest risk in the sequence.
* **Iterative Thinking:** Recognizing that AI building is a loop, not a straight line; models require constant retraining.
