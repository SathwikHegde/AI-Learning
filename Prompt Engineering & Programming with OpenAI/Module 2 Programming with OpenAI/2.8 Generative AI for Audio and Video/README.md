# Module 2.8: Generative AI for Audio and Video

This directory explores the multimodal frontiers of Artificial Intelligence, specifically focusing on **Audio and Video Generation**. Building on our work with text and images, this unit demonstrates how to programmatically generate speech, clone voices, and create dynamic video content using state-of-the-art Generative AI APIs.

---

## Learning Objectives
The primary goal of this unit is to master the integration of sound and motion into the AI development stack. Key focus areas include:
* **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Converting written text into natural-sounding human speech with control over voice, tone, and pacing.
* **Speech-to-Text (Transcription):** Leveraging models like OpenAI’s **Whisper** to accurately transcribe and translate audio files.
* **Generative Video:** Understanding the current capabilities of AI to generate video clips from text prompts or static images.
* **Business Applications:** Exploring use cases in automated customer support (IVR), localized marketing videos, and content accessibility.

---

## Key Concepts Covered

### **1. Audio Intelligence: OpenAI Whisper & TTS**
We explore how to handle audio data programmatically:
* **Transcription:** Using `Whisper` to convert multi-language audio into structured text for analysis.
* **Voice Synthesis:** Interfacing with TTS models to create high-quality voiceovers for presentations or apps.
* **Translation:** Seamlessly translating audio from one language into text in another.

### **2. The Video Generation Frontier**
We examine the transition from static images to temporal consistency in video:
* **Text-to-Video:** Defining scenes, movements, and subjects through descriptive prompts.
* **Image-to-Video:** Animating static assets to create engaging social media content or product demos.
* **Lip-Sync & Avatars:** Using AI to synchronize a generated voice with a digital human avatar.

---

## Skills & Knowledge Acquired
* **Multimodal Workflow:** Ability to chain text, audio, and video models together (e.g., generate a script, turn it into audio, and create a video for that audio).
* **Technical Asset Management:** Handling larger file formats (MP3, MP4, WAV) within the Python/Colab environment.
* **Creative Automation:** Understanding the cost and hardware requirements for rendering AI-generated video compared to static media.
