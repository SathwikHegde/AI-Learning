# Course Introduction – Module 1

**AI for Business**

## Module Overview

Module 1 introduces the foundational concepts of **Artificial Intelligence in a business context**. This module is designed to build a shared understanding of what AI is, how it has evolved, what is realistic versus overhyped, and the key factors that influence successful AI solutions in organizations.

Learners should complete all sections **in order**, as each topic builds on the previous one.

***

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, learners will be able to:

*   Explain what Artificial Intelligence means in a business environment
*   Describe the historical evolution of AI and its major milestones
*   Distinguish between real-world AI capabilities and common misconceptions
*   Identify key factors that contribute to effective AI solutions
*   Understand the basics of machine learning at a conceptual level

***

## Module Contents

This module includes the following sections:

### 1.1 Introduction to AI for Business – Part 1

An introduction to AI concepts with a focus on business relevance, value creation, and real-world applications.

### 1.2 Introduction to AI for Business – Part 2

Continues the discussion by exploring how organizations adopt AI and how AI fits into modern business strategies.

### 1.3 Short History of AI

A concise overview of AI’s evolution, including key breakthroughs and shifts that shaped today’s AI landscape.  
**Reference material:** `M1_AIHistory.pdf`

### 1.4 What Is Real and What Is Overhyped

A practical discussion separating realistic AI capabilities from exaggerated claims and marketing hype.

### 1.5 AI Solution Factors

An overview of the critical components required for successful AI solutions, including data, people, processes, and business alignment.

### 1.6 Machine Learning

An introductory explanation of machine learning concepts, terminology, and how ML supports AI-driven decision-making.
