# Module 6: AI for Retail

### *AI for Business — Columbia University*

This directory contains the comprehensive materials for **Module 6**, focusing on how Artificial Intelligence is fundamentally transforming the retail landscape. The curriculum covers the entire value chain, from back-end supply chain logistics to personalized front-end customer experiences.

## Module Objectives

The goal of this module is to understand the strategic and technical application of AI in both brick-and-mortar and e-commerce environments. Key focus areas include:

* **Operational Efficiency:** Optimizing supply chains and inventory through predictive modeling.
* **Customer Experience:** Leveraging AI for hyper-personalization and seamless shopping.
* **Strategic Integration:** Developing a cohesive AI roadmap to unify offline and online retail operations.

| Unit | Title | Key Concepts |
| --- | --- | --- |

| **6.5** | **Store Management** | AI for physical store optimization: smart shelves, automated restocking, and robotic inventory error detection. |

| **6.7** | **The Shopping Revolution** | High-tech retail interfaces: smart mirrors, automated checkouts, and friction-less shopping experiences. |
| **6.8** | **AI Tech Market Map** | Exploring the ecosystem of retail customer service, including advanced chatbots and smart receipt systems. |
| **6.9** | **Strategic Summary** | Synthesizing AI applications into an overarching strategy to improve efficiency and the "buying experience." |

## Skills & Knowledge Acquired

* **Predictive Analytics:** Understanding how to use historical and social data for demand forecasting.
* **Computer Vision Applications:** Literacy in how sensors and cameras optimize shelf management and checkout.
* **Marketing Automation:** Knowledge of how ML models process big data to create individual-level marketing.
* **Omni-channel Strategy:** Ability to design systems that synchronize the digital and physical retail experience.
