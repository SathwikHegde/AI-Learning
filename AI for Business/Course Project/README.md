# 🏗️ AI for Business: Project Space

### *Columbia University | Applied AI Implementation*

This repository serves as my professional project space and sandbox for the **AI for Business** course at Columbia University. It bridges the gap between high-level business strategy and technical execution, focusing on the end-to-end lifecycle of AI solutions.

---

##  Project Mission

The goal of this space is to move beyond theory by implementing the "sequence of building AI solutions" as taught in the curriculum. Every project here is designed to solve a specific business problem using data-driven insights and Machine Learning.

##  Tech Stack & Skills Applied

* **Language:** Python (Basics to Data Processing)
* **Libraries:** `pandas`, `numpy`, `scikit-learn` (for ML modeling)
* **Tools:** SQL, GitHub (Version Control)
* **Domain Focus:** NLP (Text Classification), Data Cleaning for Structured/Unstructured Data, and Business KPI Alignment.

---

##  Featured Implementation Areas

### **1. AI in Industry Verticals**

* **Finance:** Analyzing implementation hurdles like data silos and regulatory compliance.
* **Customer Service:** Building prototypes for automated text classification and conversational logic.
* **Retail & Healthcare:** Exploring predictive modeling for inventory and diagnostic data.

### **2. The AI "Build" Sequence**

* **Data Processing:** Pipelines for cleaning and preparing raw business data.
* **Model Evaluation:** Testing algorithms to ensure they meet business-specific "explainability" and accuracy standards.
* **Team Strategy:** Documenting the organizational requirements to scale these technical solutions.
