Module 7: AI for Healthcare
AI for Business — Columbia University
This final module explores the transformative impact of Artificial Intelligence on the healthcare sector. The curriculum focuses on how data-driven technologies are revolutionizing patient care, medical diagnostics, and the operational efficiency of healthcare systems.

Module Objectives
The goal of this module is to navigate the high-stakes environment of healthcare through an AI lens. Key focus areas include:

Diagnostic Innovation: Understanding AI's role in medical imaging and early disease detection.

Operational Optimization: Improving hospital workflows, patient access, and cost management.

Ethics & Trust: Addressing the critical importance of privacy, data security, and explainability in clinical settings.

Future Trends: Exploring multimodal AI and drug discovery.
