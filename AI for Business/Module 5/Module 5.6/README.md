Module 5: AI for Finance
 * 5.6 AI for Finance pt6: Implementation Challenges
   * This lecture examines the critical hurdles financial institutions must overcome to successfully deploy AI. Key obstacles discussed include siloed data architectures, complex regulatory compliance, the need for model explainability, and the limitations of legacy infrastructure. It also highlights the strategic risks of lacking a cohesive AI roadmap and the industry-wide shortage of specialized talent.
