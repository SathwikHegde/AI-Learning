# AI for Business – Course Notes

## Overview

These course notes accompany the **AI for Business** program and are designed to help learners understand how artificial intelligence concepts are applied in real-world business contexts. The material focuses on **practical understanding, decision-making, and strategic use of AI**, rather than deep technical implementation.

The notes can be used for:

*   Reviewing key concepts
*   Reinforcing learning after each module
*   Preparing for quizzes and discussions
*   Applying AI ideas to business scenarios

***

## Learning Objectives

By reviewing these course notes, learners will be able to:

*   Understand core artificial intelligence concepts in a business context
*   Identify common AI use cases across industries
*   Recognize opportunities where AI can improve efficiency and decision-making
*   Interpret AI-driven insights without requiring a technical background
*   Communicate effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders about AI

***

## Course Topics Covered

The course notes include explanations, examples, and summaries related to:

*   Introduction to Artificial Intelligence for Business
*   AI vs. Automation vs. Analytics
*   Data fundamentals and AI readiness
*   Prompt engineering and AI interaction basics
*   Business use cases for AI (operations, marketing, finance, real estate, etc.)
*   Ethical considerations and limitations of AI
*   Evaluating AI tools for business value

***

## How to Use These Notes

*   Read alongside the learning modules for better comprehension
