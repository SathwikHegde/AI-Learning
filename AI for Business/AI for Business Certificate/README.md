##  Certification Requirements

To earn the official **AI for Business Certificate** from **Columbia University**, the following milestones must be successfully completed. This repository documents the progress and mastery of these requirements.

### **Completion Criteria**

* **Curriculum Engagement:** Mandatory viewing of all learning pages, including Instructor Insights, Discussion Etiquette, and Academic Honesty protocols.
* **Knowledge Assessments:** Successfully passing all graded quizzes with a minimum score of **60%**.
* *Note: The course allows for unlimited attempts to ensure a deep understanding of the material, with the highest score recorded.*


* **Self-Reflections:** Submission of all self-check questions and participation in ungraded module discussions to further explore the intersection of AI and business strategy.

### **Verified Skills**

The issuance of this certificate validates proficiency in:

* **Strategic AI Implementation:** Identifying business problems solvable by Machine Learning.
* **Technical Foundations:** Utilizing basic Python for data processing and model deployment.
* **Industry Application:** Deploying AI solutions across Finance, Retail, Healthcare, and Customer Service sectors.
* **AI Leadership:** Structuring and managing technical teams for enterprise-level AI projects.
